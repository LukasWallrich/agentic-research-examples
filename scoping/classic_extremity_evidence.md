# Novelty check: anchoring vs anchor extremity (classic papers) - working notes

## Strack & Mussweiler 1997 JPSP 73:437-446, doi 10.1037/0022-3514.73.3.437 (verified Crossref)
- Study 3 (n=67, within-subject, 8 items, Latin square): plausible anchors ~1 SD from calibration mean (n=151), implausible >10 SD. Table 5 p.442 anchors; Table 6 p.443 z-values: plausible H .04 / L -.13 (diff .17); implausible H .25 / L -.17 (diff .42). Interaction F(1,66)=3.55 p<.07. Appendix p.446 per-item raw means (several rows implausible: Einstein, Gandhi, Da Vinci reversed).

## Mussweiler & Strack 2001 Soc Cog 19:145-160, doi 10.1521/soco.19.2.145.20705 (verified)
- Study 1 Antarctic mean temp (true -68): anchors -43, -17 (plausible, ~1SD), 700, 900 (implausible, 56 & 72 SD). Table 1 p.154: -41.12, -24.84, -23.27, -24.44. n 15-16/cell.
- Study 2 Gandhi age (true 78): anchors 61, 86 (+-1SD), 214, 271 (18, 26 SD). Table 2 p.156: 62.92, 70.36, 75.26, 72.86. n 26-28/cell.

## Wegener, Petty, Detweiler-Bedell & Jarvis 2001 JESP 37:62-69, doi 10.1006/jesp.2000.1431 (verified)
- 8 items, within-subject 2x2; z-standardized estimates. Exp1A (n=151) moderate H/L +.50/-.52 vs extreme +.37/-.36; Exp1B (n=145) moderate +.52/-.41 vs very extreme +.15/-.25; Exp2 (n=80, no 'random' instruction) moderate +.40/-.47 vs very extreme +.26/-.19 (p.65-66). Plausibility ratings 1.28 vs 4.38 (9-pt). Appendix p.68 anchors (6 levels per item).

## Wegener, Petty, Blankenship & Detweiler-Bedell 2010 JCP 20:5-16, doi 10.1016/j.jcps.2009.12.003 (verified)
- No new numbers. Footnote 1 p.7 and p.12: cites Wegener, Blankenship, Detweiler-Bedell & Petty (2009) raw data 'Knowledge and anchor extremity': smaller effects with low- and high-extremity than moderate; downturn earlier for high-knowledge.

## Bystranowski et al. 2021 LHB 45:1-23, doi 10.1037/lhb0000438 (verified)
- Table 4 p.12 (control vs anchor): low k=4 d=.43; medium k=42 d=.59; high k=5 d=.19; continuous coef -.04 (z), -.08 (d). Table 5 p.13 (high vs low): low k=5 d=.82; medium k=25 d=1.00; high k=7 d=.77. Anchor value = z of anchor magnitude between studies, binned at 25th/75th pct.

## Schultze et al. 2015 JDM 10:144-171, doi 10.1017/s1930297500003922 (verified)
- Advice distance 5-75% (Exp1), up to 150% (Exp3). AT = -0.093 -0.003 d + 0.153 ln d (Table 1 p.151). Pooled 6 exps p.166-167: B1=-0.002, B2=0.101, peak at 63.84%. Absolute shift ~ proportional to distance (SRM), no decrease at far distances.

## Harris et al. 2019 JEP:G, doi 10.1037/xge0000644 (accepted MS)
- Table 2 p.23; anchors p.17 (8/20 C; 6000/30000 GBP) and p.27 (2/25 C; 13000/200000 GBP).

## Simmons, LeBoeuf & Nelson 2010 JPSP 99:917-932, doi 10.1037/a0021540 (Penn repository MS)
- Study 3a/3b single-anchor plausible vs implausible (Tables 2-3, MS p.47-48). Note 2 (MS p.42): implausible anchors produced larger anchoring (estimates further from truth) for 6/8 items. No means per level.

## Lieder et al. 2018 PBR 25:322-349, doi 10.3758/s13423-017-1286-8 (preprint version)
- Eq 3-4: E[x^] = a r^t + (1-r^t) x -> constant relative adjustment.
